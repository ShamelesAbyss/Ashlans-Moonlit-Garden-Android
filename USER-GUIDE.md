# Evening guide

## Your first evening

Choose Moonmint, tap an empty bed to plant, then tend it again to water. A Moonmint takes 22 active garden seconds to mature. Gather it for 2 mooncoins. Emberbell takes 32 seconds; Ashlan’s Bloom takes 42. Up to nine beds can be tended, and nothing withers. Rain waters planted crops. Resting begins a new evening, refreshes woodland patches, and matures crops that were already watered.

Visit the Hushwood to gather eight Ghostcap patches and four Duskthorn sprigs per evening. The Violet Cabinet has six fictional recipes. Pick a recipe, then perform Prepare, Stir, and Bottle; ingredients are only consumed at bottling. Nettle’s one-time parcel helps you get started. Bramble sells three seeds at a time and three levels of enchanted soil improve crop growth.

Talk to Nettle, scratch behind her ears, give her Moonmint, or chase a spark. The glade accepts lanterns, flowers, and paper stars. Ashlan’s Bloom wakes the moonflowers there.

## Jukebox and sound

The Enchanted Jukebox uses the local Media3 player. It supports play/pause, previous/next, shuffle, repeat-one/all, track selection, seeking, and a separate soundtrack volume. The public playlist is currently empty: the original recording and its cover remain in the private reference project until their public redistribution rights are documented. No replacement track is bundled.

Soundtrack, ambience, and interaction-effect settings are stored separately. Playback is paused when the app leaves the foreground and does not resume without a tap.

## Journal and privacy

Journal pages and unfinished drafts are kept in private on-device app storage. Pages are append-only in the interface. The game never invents personal memories or messages from Ashlan. The app requests no Internet permission, account, cloud service, telemetry, or advertising.

Use **More → Settings → Export JSON backup** to save a portable file through Android’s file picker. Keep a copy somewhere you control. Import accepts a validated version-1 save and replaces progress/settings only after confirmation; current journal pages are merged by exact date-and-text match, and a pre-import recovery save is retained locally. **Restore pre-import recovery copy** can switch back to that prior progress while preserving journal pages added since.

The new app has its own application ID and can coexist with the previous edition. Its development APK is debug-signed, not production-signed. Debug signing can change between builds; Android may not permit an in-place update. Export and verify a backup before uninstalling any app or changing signing identity.
